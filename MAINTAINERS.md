# Maintainers

Maintainers have merge rights for specific topic areas to avoid single bottlenecks.

| Handle | Topic area(s) | Credibility note |
|---|---|---|
| your-github-handle | board-representation, search | Engine author (~2100+ CCRL) |

To become a maintainer, submit a few content PRs first. Then open a PR adding your handle with a link to your engine or GitHub profile.
